# Chapter 9 — Perimeter, area and volume

Cambridge Lower Secondary Mathematics, Stage 7 foundations: metric units, perimeter, areas of rectangles and triangles, compound shapes, and volume and surface area of cuboids. See [source and scope](README.md).

## Key ideas

### Metric units

Length: 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, 1000 m = 1 km. Mass: 1000 g = 1 kg. Capacity: 1000 ml = 1 litre. To change to a smaller unit, multiply; to change to a larger unit, divide: 3.5 m = 350 cm and 2400 g = 2.4 kg.

### Perimeter

The perimeter is the total distance around the edge of a shape. Add the lengths of all the sides, including any you have to work out from the others.

### Area of a rectangle and a triangle

The area of a rectangle is length × width. A triangle is half of a rectangle with the same base and height, so its area is 1/2 × base × height. The height is measured at right angles to the base, not along a sloping side. Area is in square units such as cm² and m².

### Compound shapes

Split a compound shape into rectangles and triangles, find each area, and add them. Or work out a larger rectangle and subtract the piece that is missing.

### Volume of a cuboid

Volume is the space inside a solid, in cubic units such as cm³. The volume of a cuboid is length × width × height. A cube with edge a has volume a³. 1 cm³ holds 1 ml, so 1000 cm³ = 1 litre.

### Surface area of a cuboid

The surface area is the total area of all six faces. A cuboid has three pairs of equal faces: front and back, top and bottom, and the two sides.

## Key facts and formulas

| Fact or method | What it means | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| 10 mm = 1 cm, 100 cm = 1 m, 1000 m = 1 km | Length units | Converting lengths. |
| 1000 g = 1 kg, 1000 ml = 1 litre | Mass and capacity units | Converting masses and capacities. |
| Area of rectangle = l × w | Length times width | Rectangles and squares. |
| Area of triangle = 1/2 × b × h | Half the matching rectangle | Any triangle, with its perpendicular height. |
| Volume of cuboid = l × w × h | Layers of unit cubes | Boxes, tanks and rooms. |
| 1 cm³ = 1 ml | Volume to capacity | How much a container holds. |

## Using the methods in different situations

### Situation: converting units

**Example.** 4.2 km = 4.2 × 1000 = 4200 m.

**Example — capacity.** 750 ml = 750 ÷ 1000 = 0.75 litres.

**Example — mass.** 2400 g = 2400 ÷ 1000 = 2.4 kg.

### Situation: perimeter and area

**Example — a rectangle.** A rectangle is 9 cm by 4 cm. Its perimeter is 9 + 4 + 9 + 4 = 26 cm and its area is 9 × 4 = 36 cm².

**Example — from the area.** A square has an area of 49 cm². Its side is 7 cm, so its perimeter is 4 × 7 = 28 cm.

**Example — a triangle.** A triangle has a base of 10 cm and a height of 6 cm. Its area is 1/2 × 10 × 6 = 30 cm².

### Situation: compound shapes

**Example — subtracting a corner.** An L-shape is a 10 m by 8 m rectangle with a 4 m by 3 m rectangle cut from one corner. Area = 80 − 12 = 68 m².

**Example — its perimeter.** Cutting a rectangle from a corner does not change the perimeter: the L-shape's perimeter is still 2 × (10 + 8) = 36 m.

**Example — adding parts.** A shape is a 6 cm by 5 cm rectangle with a triangle of base 6 cm and height 4 cm on top. Area = 30 + 1/2 × 6 × 4 = 30 + 12 = 42 cm².

### Situation: volume and capacity

**Example.** A cuboid is 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm. Its volume is 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 cm³.

**Example — a tank.** A fish tank is 30 cm by 20 cm by 10 cm. Its volume is 6000 cm³, so it holds 6000 ml = 6 litres.

**Example — a cube.** A cube has edges of 3 cm. Its volume is 3³ = 27 cm³.

### Situation: surface area

**Example.** The 5 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm cuboid has faces of 5 × 4 = 20, 5 × 3 = 15 and 4 × 3 = 12 cm², each twice. Surface area = 2 × 20 + 2 × 15 + 2 × 12 = 40 + 30 + 24 = 94 cm².

## Common mistakes

- Adding the length and width for an area instead of multiplying them.
- Mixing units, such as multiplying metres by centimetres: change to one unit first.
- Using a sloping side as the height of a triangle.
- Forgetting the 1/2 in the area of a triangle.
- Writing a volume in cm² or an area in cm³.
